Renderer

Renderer

Direct3D 11 (the default) and Vulkan both draw on your graphics card. If the game shows a problem on your PC with one, try the other. Software works on any PC but is slow: use it only if nothing shows with either.

Vulkan compatibility

For the newest graphics cards and drivers (3D black while menus show). Auto turns it on for the GeForce RTX 50 series.

GPU

Quality

Game quality

Anti-aliasing

FXAA smooths jagged edges over the whole picture, at almost no cost.

Image

These and the rest of this page work on their own, on top of the game quality. Game default keeps the game as it is.

Render resolution

The game draws at this share of the window and the picture is scaled up. Lower is faster on a weak graphics card; 100% is sharp even when the game quality asks for less.

Upscaling

How a picture drawn below the window's resolution is enlarged. FSR 1 is the sharpest, SGSR is lighter. With FXAA on, the picture is enlarged plainly.

Detail

Anisotropic filtering

Sharper floors and walls seen at an angle, at little cost.

Texture quality

Half or quarter loads smaller textures: less memory and faster loading on a weak PC, at the cost of detail.

Effects

The game's own effects, turned off one by one in every quality level once its title shows. Each one off gives the graphics card less to draw; Game default keeps them as the quality level sets them.

Reflections

Bloom

Frame rate

Frame rate

The game's limit. Higher needs more of the computer.

VSync

On: no tearing, the frame rate follows the screen's. Off: no wait for the screen.

Idle frame limit

After 10 seconds without a key, click or stick, the game draws at most this many frames a second: cooler and quieter while it waits. Any input brings the full rate back.

Game code

Compiled translates the game's code once, in the background, and gets faster as you play.

Performance overlay

Frame rate, frame times, CPU and GPU, in the game's log every second (and on screen). In the game, F9 shows or hides it and F8 records a 30-second summary.

Language

Text

Voices

Applied when the game starts. In-game keeps the game's own choice. This version shows its text in English or Korean; Japanese is for the voices.

Changes to the game rules, made by the local server (Off is the original game). They apply each time the game starts and never undo what was already earned.

Shop and summons

Unlimited shop stock

Shop items that restock daily, weekly or monthly never run out, gems and summon tickets included. Gem packs cost nothing on this server.

Summon tickets become gems

No duplicate ★4

A ★4 costume or weapon you already own is swapped for one you don't, from the same summon.

Pity rewards

Every 100 summon medals become 1,000 gems and 100 Mama Points, sent to the gift box.

Lost Items give gems

Hide limited-time sale notices

Stops the "on sale for a limited time · Ends In" messages that repeat on the home screen. Applies the next time the game starts.

EXP

Your progress is kept in save files. Start over in a new one, switch between them, and change what a save owns. Close the game first: saves change only while it is closed, and a copy of a save is kept before every change.

Save files

Reading the saves...

Start over makes a new save and plays it from the beginning; the save you have now is kept and can be used again at any time.

Owning a costume unlocks its character. Weapons are listed in their first form: evolve them in the game. Levels above what a costume or weapon can reach now ascend it as far as needed (no materials are used). Owned things are not taken away.

Logs

Export logs saves them in one zip to send with a bug report, with your system and GPU details. Your Windows user and PC names are left out.

Graphics diagnostics

Writes graphics details to the game's log: turn it on when the 3D does not show, play until it happens, then send the zip from Export logs.
